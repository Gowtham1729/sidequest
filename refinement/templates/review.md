## Primary findings (up to three)

For each: finding ID; severity; exact reproduction; expected and actual outcome; artifact/file pointer; player impact; acceptance test for the repair. Distinguish a demonstrated defect from a hypothesis.

## Other findings and optional ideas

Optional ideas do not block an otherwise passing ticket. Missing required evidence does.

## Decision

Accept / repair / evidence pending, with a short criterion-based reason. Identify the single most useful next action. Do not invent a numerical quality rating or add an unrelated rewrite requirement.
